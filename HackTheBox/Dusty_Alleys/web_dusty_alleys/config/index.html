The Underground Labyrinth

As the survivors venture deeper into the underground labyrinth, the oppressive darkness envelops them, and the air grows thick with dust and decay. The maze-like passages twist and turn, confounding their senses and sapping their resolve.

Just as hope begins to wane and despair threatens to take hold, a faint glimmer of light catches their attention. Through the gloom, they spot a small, dilapidated robot, its metal frame battered and rusted from years of neglect. It emits feeble sparks, its circuits struggling to maintain functionality. So they decided to get the answers from him.
